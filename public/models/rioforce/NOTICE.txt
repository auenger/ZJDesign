Adaptation for the Zhuojiang model laboratory:
- Converted the source Blender scene into static glTF 2.0 binary (GLB).
- Used the rig's rest pose and excluded the original floor, lights, camera and rig.
- Grouped loose mesh islands into ten independent physical components.
- Evaluated subdivision at levels 1 and 2; no replacement procedural human geometry.
- Rebuilt legacy Cycles shaders as PBR, preserving the original UV atlas and cap color.
- Applied Draco geometry compression. Original texture remains embedded in GLB.
- Viewer provides optional metal shading, wireframe, and exploded presentation.

Conversion recipe: scripts/convert-minifigure.py in the project repository.
File sizes, triangle counts and SHA-256 hashes: manifest.json.
